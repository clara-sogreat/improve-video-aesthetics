# Example: a mechanical garden

This is a planning example, not a record of generated or verified results.

## Brief

Create a 35-second film in which an adult woman discovers a mechanical flower opening in a quiet garden. Preserve her chin-length dark hair, ivory dress and brown boots. The waist-high flower has five brass petals and stands to her right. The audience should feel gentle surprise.

## Shared art card

Blue-gray surroundings, warm brass accents, ceramic surfaces and soft back-left light. Keep layered depth and uncluttered silhouettes. Exclude neon clutter and plastic skin. Identity, wardrobe and the flower's structure remain fixed.

## Still prompt

An adult woman with chin-length dark hair, an ivory dress and brown boots stands beside a waist-high five-petal brass mechanical flower on her right, in a quiet blue-gray ceramic garden. Medium framing, layered depth, soft warm back-left light, readable metal and ceramic textures, uncluttered silhouettes.

Compare the baseline with one changed style setting. Record actual observations and preservation of the required details; until generation occurs, mark every outcome pending. Do not invent profile IDs.

| Time | Purpose | Still / motion intent | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | Establish space | Wide garden / gentle forward camera | Clear geography |
| 6–11 s | Establish identity | Medium character / slight head turn | Stable face and clothing |
| 11–16 s | Reveal mechanism | Flower detail / petals begin opening | Five readable petals |
| 16–23 s | Approach | Woman beside flower / one short step | Stable body and direction |
| 23–30 s | Interaction | Hand near flower / gentle touch | Coherent contact and prop |
| 30–35 s | Reaction | Close reaction / restrained smile | Identity and story clarity |

Approve assets first. Test a short hand/prop interaction before committing to the whole sequence. If motion fails, simplify the action or repair the starting asset. Stills alone cannot validate the interaction.
