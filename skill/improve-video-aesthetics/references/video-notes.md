# Method notes and provenance

This workflow paraphrases methods from a supplied visual-style tutorial and adds reusable production and delivery checks. The original tutorial, original author's skill package, account profiles and private style-reference IDs are not included. This package does not claim to reproduce an unavailable private setup.

## Tutorial-derived ideas

- Complete content prompts make style comparisons more useful.
- Keep content fixed when comparing aesthetic settings.
- Personalization, moodboards, style references and identity references serve different purposes.
- Select preferences by visible attributes that fit the project.
- Test both characters and environments before expanding a sequence.
- Preserve approved art direction through image-to-video; evaluate staging and performance separately.

A demonstrated 100/0/0 baseline and stylization 500 comparison are illustrative settings, not platform limits or universally optimal values. Selection counts and elapsed time are anecdotes rather than requirements. Verify current provider capabilities before using exact parameters.

## Added workflow guidance

Shared art cards, explicit content invariants, comparison records, asset approval, separate still/motion briefs, continuity checks and delivery evidence make the method reusable. Instrumental-only music requires listening to actual audio rather than trusting prompt tags.

No success rate, cost reduction, viral outcome or universal quality improvement is claimed. A production example demonstrates application, not a controlled benchmark.
