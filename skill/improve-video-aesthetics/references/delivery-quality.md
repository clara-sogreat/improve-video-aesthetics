# Delivery quality

## Music acceptance

Translate the brief into instruments, rhythm, melody, dynamics and space. A no-vocals tag is a goal, not evidence that a generated track contains no human voices, choir or vocal-like sounds.

Audition the actual candidate before integrating it when instrumental-only music is required. If the agent cannot hear audio, provide a preview and obtain the user's listening judgment; keep that check pending until then. Level statistics cannot prove the absence of vocals.

When a candidate fails, change the approach before repeatedly integrating full exports. Alternatives include an auditioned licensed instrumental or an original arrangement rendered with explicitly instrumental sources. Describe the provider and production method truthfully; a fallback is not evidence that the rejected model succeeded.

Preserve the approved track and its hash. Do not add unrequested voices or layers. Redistribute finished music only when its rights permit; instrument sample libraries are not automatically redistributable.

## Export review

Check decode, duration, frame count, resolution, audio format, caption readability and scene transitions. Review representative opening, middle, ending and action-turn frames, but do not claim full motion playback from sampled frames.

Compare final audio with the approved source. AAC encoding can introduce delay: measure alignment before declaring the wrong track was used. If repairing audio only, preserve the approved picture stream where appropriate and verify the resulting export again.

Actually audition the final export for listening claims; report user audition separately from agent checks. Keep passed, failed and pending evidence distinct. Technical checks do not establish aesthetic success, causal improvement, guaranteed reach or legal ownership of third-party assets.

Paid generation and public posting require user authorization. A skill installation alone does not provide it.
